# `assets-src/` artwork exchange

**Rule.** The founder drops new artwork at the root of `assets-src/`; the agent renames it, optimises it into `assets/`, files the master under the shipped name and leaves the root empty.

**Why.** Founder workflow: exports arrive under arbitrary names; tidying them is the agent's job every time.

**How.** Ship the optimised file under `assets/` with a self-describing name; keep the master in `assets-src/` under that same name.
