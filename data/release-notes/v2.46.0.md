NFC tools to format chips and write links, Wi‑Fi or contacts onto any NFC tag, maker chips of every brand linked to your spools, and a new app icon.

## Added

- **NFC tools.** Press "+" in the search bar, then NFC tools. While it is open, placing a spool on the TigerPOD opens nothing.
  - **Format your RFID chip** turns the chips on the TigerPOD back into blank TigerTags, ready for a new spool.
  - **Write in NDEF** puts a link, a Wi‑Fi network, a contact, an e‑mail, a phone number or an SMS on any NFC tag, readable by any phone. A link can be a website, a local address (192.168.0.1) or a camera or data stream (rtsp://…). **Reset NDEF** empties a tag.
  - The room left is shown as you type and follows the chip: NTAG213, NTAG215 and NTAG216 are all supported.
  - The last link and your contact card are remembered for next time.
- **Auto mode.** Turn on Auto on "Format" or "Write to the chip", then place the chips one after another: each one is written at once, with a beep. A spool of your inventory is never written this way.
- **Burn a TigerTag+ from the catalogue.** Every product card of the catalogue has the "Burn the TigerTag+" banner: hold it with blank chips on the TigerPOD.
- **FlashForge AD5X, Creator 5 and Creator 5 Pro** in the Klipper printer list.

## Changed

- **Maker chips of every brand.** Bambu Lab, Creality, Qidi, Anycubic and every other spool with a MIFARE Classic 1K chip can be linked. A spool can keep its 2 TigerTag chips and its 2 maker chips, or only the maker chips: placing it on the TigerPOD opens it either way.
- **Upgrade to TigerTag+ or TigerData+ from the catalogue.** The banner on the spool opens the catalogue beside it; pick the product and hold to upgrade. A TigerTag only upgrades with its own chips on the TigerPOD, which are rewritten right after.
- **New app icon** on macOS, Windows and Linux.
- "Please update your TigerTag" is now a banner you hold, like the burn.
- "+" in the search bar opens and closes the add window, and "Link to my inventory" is always there.
- Entering a weight by hand happens in place, without moving the card. "Balance" is now "On scale".
- "+ Material" on a material's card is held 1 second, so a stray click no longer adds a spool.
- The colour of a material has its own row on its card.
- Snapmaker U1 on the Paxx firmware: the latest firmware is offered even offline.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where an old spool appeared in the inventory when a chip was placed while the add window was open.
- Fixed an issue where an NFC tag written by a phone was added to the inventory as a spool.
- Fixed an issue where upgrading a TigerTag to TigerTag+ stopped before rewriting the chips, and "API refresh failed" appeared (#154).
- Fixed an issue where a spool left without a container could not get one again (#154).
- Fixed an issue where recording a Bambu Lab camera stopped after about 10 seconds. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012.
- Fixed an issue where adding a FlashForge printer said the serial number was wrong when LAN mode was off (#86).
- Fixed an issue where a USB scale could stop answering after the computer woke from sleep (#138).
