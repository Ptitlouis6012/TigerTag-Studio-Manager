Tiger Touch arrives in Tiger Studio, demo printers anyone can try, screenshots of your TigerTag devices straight into a report, and requests that land on the right project.

## Added

- Tiger Touch: the BIGTREETECH Panda Touch / K-Touch screen running the Tiger Touch firmware now has its own card. See its status, Wi-Fi, firmware and the printers it follows — with whether the screen itself reaches each one. Watch the screen live and use it with your mouse (a click is a tap, a drag is a swipe), or take a screenshot to save, copy or attach to a report. No screen yet? The card links to the web installer.
- New achievement: a Tiger Touch earns 100 XP.
- Demo printers: try Tiger Studio without a printer. Pick any of about 400 models, across Bambu Lab, Creality, Elegoo, FlashForge, Snapmaker, Anycubic, Prusa, Klipper and OctoPrint, and it prints on its own on the brand's real connection — temperatures, filament slots, camera, pause and stop. Each demo matches its model: an A1 mini has no AMS, an H2D has two nozzles, a Prusa XL five heads.
- TigerScale V3: live view and screenshot of the scale's screen from its card (requires the scale's new firmware). Click the picture to use the scale.
- TigerSpool: screenshot of the box's screen from its card.
- Requests: pick what your request is about first — Tiger Studio, Tiger Touch, Tiger POD, Tiger Scale, Tiger Spool or TigerTag RFID Connect — and it is filed on that project. Tiger Touch, Tiger Scale and Tiger Spool requests can capture the device's screen in one click, and Tiger Touch and Tiger Spool requests include the printers the device follows.
- Klipper printers: every extra temperature the printer reports (chamber, electronics, dryer…) in a Sensors section, and your Mainsail theme logo in place of the default one. Thanks to @storm8488.
- Klipper and OctoPrint: the Add a printer card lists the models with their pictures and a search.
- Remaining filament length next to the weight of a spool, for example 353 g ≈ 141 m.

## Changed

- Klipper and OctoPrint printers: click a temperature to set it, like the other brands.
- Requests are now sent by TigerSquad members. Joining is free, and the request card opens the TigerSquad presentation when needed.
- Annotating a screenshot now happens in a window over the app instead of full screen.
- Updated product catalogue.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where Klipper and OctoPrint cameras overflowed their frame.
- Fixed an issue where a FlashForge printer opened right after being added showed no camera until its card was reopened.
- Fixed an issue where some printer and device access codes could appear in the information shared with AI assistants.
