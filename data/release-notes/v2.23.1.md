Your TigerScale card now looks like the scale itself, Creality filament colours are back on the printer board, and spools kept without a masterspool finally have a home.

## Added

- Added a **Refill** option at the top of the container list, for a spool you keep with no masterspool at all. It weighs nothing, so the weight shown is the filament's own.

## Changed

- The TigerScale card now mirrors the scale's own screen: the weight on the left, the container and filament values on the right, and — new — the shelf and slot where that spool is stored.
- Tare is now a key beneath the screen, inside the card, exactly where it sits on the scale itself. It shows the same two-line face as the device.
- The Tare button is now labelled the way the scale labels it, in every language.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where every filament slot of a Creality CFS unit appeared grey on the printer board, even though the printer's own panel showed the right colours.
- Fixed an issue where changing language left an open Scales panel in the previous one.
- Fixed an issue where the scale's name and the Tare label were invisible in light mode.
- Fixed an issue where holding the Tare button showed no progress in light mode, making it look like nothing was happening.
