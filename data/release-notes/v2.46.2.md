Account switching on the Deals page in about a second, a catalogue that syncs in a second and keeps your spools' photos up to date, no more flicker on Mac.

## Changed

- **Deals load in about a second when you switch accounts.** They used to take up to a minute, with the page reloading several times.
- **The catalogue syncs in about a second.** A large catalogue no longer fails to sync.
- **Photos follow the catalogue.** When a brand changes a product photo, the spools linked to that product show the new one after the next catalogue sync. A photo you set yourself is never replaced.
- **Photos load at the size they are shown.** Grids, lists and side cards download smaller pictures, so they appear faster and use less memory.
- **"Give an envelope"** has a new look: the envelope follows the mouse, and "Give" is held for 2 seconds to send.
- The window opened by "+" shows the logos of the RFID chips and printers Tiger Studio works with.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where parts of the window flickered or went blank on Retina screens (macOS).
- Fixed an issue where the catalogue grid kept showing old product photos.
- Fixed an issue where refreshing a spool's catalogue data replaced a photo you had set yourself.
