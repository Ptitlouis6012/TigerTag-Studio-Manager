Set your own tax rate, add a Canadian one, and connect the two newest Bambu Lab machines. Plus a round of fixes to how product pictures load.

## Added

- **Set your own tax rate.** A new *Custom* choice at the bottom of the country list in your profile lets you enter a rate, a currency code and a symbol yourself — for a country the list does not carry, a reduced rate, or an exemption.
- **Canada, with its provincial rates.** All 13 provinces and territories, each with the combined rate a buyer actually pays, in Canadian dollars shown as `CA$` so they are never mistaken for US ones.
- **Bambu Lab A2L and X2D.** Both are recognised automatically when discovered or added through your Bambu account, and both now have a connection walkthrough.
- **A Wiki button** in the sidebar, next to Discord and the rest.

## Changed

- The **Search** view is now called **Catalogue** — which is what it always was: the official TigerTag+ product catalogue, beside your own stock.
- **Scanning a barcode in your inventory now lands on one spool.** A scan used to return every spool whose code merely started the same way — with some brands, dozens of them.
- The inventory search box now says it can find a **reference or a barcode**. It always could.
- **A rack shelf can be four spools deep**, up from three.
- The **X1E** now shows an X1E in the add-printer form instead of an X1C.
- The bundled product database ships fresher, so a new install starts with a current catalogue.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where product pictures in the catalogue gave up after a single failed load and left a flat colour in their place for the rest of the session.
- Fixed the catalogue downloading pictures far larger than the space it draws them in, and all at once — it now asks for the size it needs and loads them in order as you scroll.
- Fixed an issue where opening the add-a-printer panel while a printer's card was open put the brand list behind it, out of sight, so the click looked ignored.
- Fixed community notification icons showing a black logo in dark mode instead of a white one.
- Fixed a Bambu Lab H2C not being recognised when it reported one of its two model codes.
