# v2.21.0

## Added

- **Racks can now be deep** — up to three rows of spools, one behind the other. Storage draws them in perspective so the spool at the back stays readable, and a **Depth** button lets you set how far apart the rows are drawn. That spacing follows your account, so it greets you the same on any computer you sign in from.
- **Arrange your racks where you actually keep them.** Grab a rack by its header and drag it anywhere — no mode to switch on. Racks snap to each other as you move them, and a thin guide shows what they caught. Nothing else on the board moves. **Arrange** mode is still there for resizing a rack, with a dashed outline showing the size you are heading for while you drag.
- **Find a colour by pointing at it.** Two bars above your inventory — a rainbow, and a white-to-black ramp for greys — with a bubble you drag along them. Spools in range stay lit, the rest fade, so you see *where* on the shelf that orange actually is. Pull an edge to widen the range, roll the wheel inside the bubble to fine-tune it, double-click it to let go. It works in Inventory, Favorites, the order list, Search and Storage, and it remembers your choice as you move between them.
- **Type to find in the filter menus.** Brand, Material, Aspect, Type and Tag now open with a search field: type a few letters and the list narrows. It matches anywhere in the name, so "sun" finds both Sunlu and eSun.
- **A few new sounds** — a chime when a spool joins your inventory, and a tick and a thunk when you pick a rack up and set it down.

## Changed

- **One add button instead of two.** The **+** now lives inside the search bar, and it knows where you are: it offers the two ways to add material in your inventory, the printer brands in Printers, and a new rack in Storage.
- **One button clears everything.** The refresh icon on the left of the search bar resets the search, every filter menu and the colour bars at once. It stays greyed out while there is nothing to clear.
- **Not stored** now lists your most recent spools first — what you have just scanned is what you are about to put away. Empty spools still sit at the bottom.
- Racks are no longer reordered by dragging a handle: where a rack sits is where you put it on the plan.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where a spool's chip was written with its remaining weight multiplied by a thousand — a 1 kg spool showing as 1 000 000 g. Anything read from a chip is now also capped at the spool's own capacity, so an impossible figure can never enter your stock.
- Fixed an issue where the Brand menu listed printer brands instead of filament brands after visiting Printers, leaving no way to filter your spools by brand.
- Fixed an issue where a rack's **⋮** menu opened far away from its rack when the plan was zoomed out.
- Fixed a disconnected scale still showing a battery level it could no longer know.
- Fixed the selected view being impossible to make out in dark mode.
- Fixed views starting at slightly different distances from the filters depending on which one you were in.
- Fixed three filter menus being able to sit open on top of each other.
- Fixed a spool moved between the front and back rows of the same column not appearing to move.
- Fixed back rows being impossible to reach in picture view, and being drawn smaller and faded instead of like the front row.
