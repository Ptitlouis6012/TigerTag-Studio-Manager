Create a spool and write its chip in one go, and a live preview that looks exactly like your inventory.

## Added

- **Create and write the chip in one go** — with a TigerPOD or an ACR122U reader plugged in, creating a material (by hand or from the catalogue) goes straight on to writing its NFC chip: a manual entry becomes a TigerTag, a catalogue product a TigerTag+. Without a reader, the new spool's card simply opens.
- **Live preview card** — when adding a material by hand, the preview is the very card it will have in your inventory, updated as you fill in the form.

## Changed

- Two-colour spools now show both colours solid with a soft blend between them; tricolour spools show a smooth colour wheel.
- In the storage view (picture mode), spool photos are framed in the spool's colour, like in the grid.
- After adding a material, the panel you used closes and the new spool's card opens.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where a catalogue spool written to a chip showed up as a TigerTag instead of a TigerTag+. Spools already affected are corrected automatically.
- Fixed an issue where the chip-writing window warned that a blank chip already held data, and blocked writing until confirmed.

## Removed

- The unused "Dual NFC" switch in the add-material form.
