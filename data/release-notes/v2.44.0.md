Tiger Squad Deals, TigerCoins and envelopes arrive in Tiger Studio: partner offers for squad members, envelopes to open and to give to your friends, a new notification centre, a redrawn Maker's Workshop and new animated banners.

## Added

- **Deals.** A new Deals view in the Tiger Squad group of the view bar, for squad members.
  - Partner offers with "Get my code": shared or single-use codes, a countdown once claimed, the stock left on limited offers, and "I used it" to move an offer to your history.
  - "All deals", "My codes" and "Exchange" tabs, with a search on title, brand and description.
  - Each offer shows its partner's website, open to every member.
  - Announcements, such as the mobile app with a QR code per platform.
- **TigerCoins and envelopes.** Every TigerTag+ Certified chip you scan gives you a surprise envelope.
  - Open them one by one or ten at a time in an animated opening, from common to legendary.
  - The TigerCoins you win can be exchanged for offers in the Exchange tab.
  - Gift codes, printed on cards or RFID chips, can be redeemed with "I have a code".
  - "How to get envelopes" lists every way to earn them.
- **Give an envelope to a friend.** From the Friends panel or the opening screen, pick a friend and hold "Give". The number of gifts per day depends on your Tiger Squad level. Your friend is notified.
- **New secret missions.** "Behind the scenes" (Discovery) and "Under the hood" (Explorer) appear once the other levels of their chapter are won.
- **XP history:** compare your curve with your country and with the leagues.
- **AI assistants on the web:** Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Le Chat can be connected from My profile, with a link to each assistant's setup guide.

## Changed

- **Notification centre** redesigned: wider, with tabs (Envelopes, Squad, System, Stock), notifications grouped by day, and one card for every spool running low.
- **Maker's Workshop** (formerly Items): illustrated loot and feats, the four legendary devices on the banner, lit once you own them.
- **Animated banners** for Deals, the Makers' League, XP history and the Maker's Workshop, with the tiger in the TigerTag cap.
- **Makers' League:** each country's row takes its flag colours, each maker's row their theme colour, and the arrows show the places won or lost over 7 days.
- **Edit my profile:** photo, display name and theme colour in their own window, with a live preview. Your theme colour now tints the header and the banners.
- **My profile** fits in two columns.
- **NFC tag tester** redesigned, with one card per reader and the chip's details at a glance.
- The "Tiger Studio pleases you?" notification lets you choose Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi or PayPal.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where stopping a Bambu Lab camera recording could freeze the app on Windows. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012.
- Fixed an issue where the daily quest "Find a filament from a brand" did not count when the product was opened from a catalogue search.
- Fixed an issue where changing the photo closed "Edit my profile".
- Fixed an issue where a Tiger Quests card could flash at random.
- Fixed an issue where switching account kept showing the previous account's TigerCoins and envelopes.
- Fixed an issue where the NFC tag tester showed "Unknown format" for every chip.
