Twin-chip spools now know they are twins, and the Snapmaker U1 camera works without custom firmware.

## Added

- Spools carrying two TigerTag chips now record which chip is which ("chip 1 of 2"). When only one of the two has been scanned, the spool card asks you to scan the other one.
- New spools encoded from the app write this chip number automatically (single chip, or chip 1 and 2 of a twin), and updating a spool's weight or colour keeps it intact.
- The chip number is included in `.ttag` exports and imports.
- Snapmaker U1: the camera now works on the original Snapmaker firmware, without the Paxx extended firmware (about one image per second). With Paxx, the smooth live video is unchanged.

## Changed

- The TigerScale photo now shows the current V3 hardware.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where deleting favorites always failed with a permission error.
- Fixed an issue where the catalogue grid flickered when adding or removing a favorite.
- Fixed an issue where, on a Snapmaker printer card, the camera stayed fixed at the top instead of scrolling with the rest of the card.
- Two chips of the same product written seconds apart are no longer paired as a twin when they say they are single chips.
- Updated a component used by automatic updates to a version with a security fix.
