Prusa printers and the Elegoo Centauri Carbon join the app (in beta), the catalogue gets its own product card, and the product card is rebuilt from the ground up.

## Added

- 🧪 Beta — Prusa printers: MK4 / MK4S, MK3.9 / MK3.9S, Core One, XL (1, 2 or 5 toolheads), MINI / MINI+, and MK3S+ / MK2.5S running PrusaLink on a Raspberry Pi. Add one by scanning your network or typing its IP, then enter the PrusaLink password shown on the printer.
- Prusa: live status (state, temperatures, progress, time left, file name and preview, fans, speed and flow), pause / resume / stop, MMU slots and XL toolheads, Buddy3D or PrusaLink camera, and automatic reconnection after an IP change.
- 🧪 Beta — Elegoo Centauri Carbon (the first one): it can now be added by its IP address, with live status, camera, and pause / resume / stop.
- Catalogue: a product opened from the catalogue gets its own card, with buttons to add it to your inventory, a wishlist, your order list or your favorites, and a button that opens the full product card beside it.
- Catalogue: when a search finds nothing, the app offers to create the spool by hand.
- Container picker: mark MasterSpools as favorites with the star. Favorites are listed first, and when you add a refill the app always lets you choose between your favorites and the brand's own MasterSpool.
- Container picker: filter the list by Cardboard, Plastic or MasterSpool.
- Spools added from the catalogue now keep the full product information (description, documents, slicer profiles, fan speed, brand website).

## Changed

- The product card is now a single card, redesigned: stock and estimated value at the top, then Information, Documents and Notes tabs, with print settings, product details, stock management and slicer profiles side by side. The Documents tab shows how many documents the product has.
- A newly added spool now gets the right container: the brand's own when there is one, otherwise you are asked to choose. A refill goes into a MasterSpool, never into "Without MasterSpool".
- Inventory cards now read like the catalogue: the product name on top (for example "PETG HF - Blue") and the brand and material below.
- Elegoo: the Centauri Carbon 2 is named correctly everywhere, and an empty network scan says which models are supported.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the inventory search found nothing for a multi-word search such as "Bambu PETG HF", while the catalogue found the product.
- Fixed an issue where the card of a catalogue product you own said "0 in stock", and where a minimum stock or a favorite set there did not apply to your spools.
- Fixed an issue on the printer board where dropping a group of printers onto another pushed the other printer's widgets away.

## Known limitations (beta)

- Prusa printers are reached on your local network only, and PrusaLink does not report which filament is loaded in each slot.
- Prusa cameras do not appear in the detached camera window yet. The Prusa logo and printer pictures are placeholders.
- Centauri Carbon (first model): pause, resume and stop are not yet confirmed on every firmware version.
