Meet the TigerSquad: the community of makers who keep open 3D printing moving. Joining is free, takes one click, and gives you 50 XP right away — your level, badge and points then grow on their own as you print.

## Added

- TigerSquad: join for free from the invitation window, the Support menu, the Improvements & suggestions card or My profile — you get 50 XP on arrival and appear on the public wall of fame at tigersystem.io.
- Four TigerSquad levels, shown as a badge and a coloured ring on your avatar, to your friends and on the wall: Bronze when you join, Silver with 20 TigerTag chips, Gold with a donation, Platinum with a donation and 50 certified TigerTag+.
- XP points for every TigerTag chip in your account — 1 per TigerTag, 2 per TigerTag+, 10 per certified TigerTag+ (checked against the factory signature). Spools already in your inventory count too, each chip only once. Your total is shown under your name and in My profile.
- A member sees their level, a thank-you note and the number of members in the Support menu and the Improvements & suggestions card, with a link to the wall.
- Support the project with Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi or PayPal from any coffee button. A donation is linked to your account (with your supporter code or your payment email, or claimed afterwards) and brings a thank-you notification.
- Refill spools show a green Refill tag on their picture in the grid, the lists, the spool details and the catalogue product card.
- A notification tells you when the team replies to one of your requests (your own comments do not trigger it).
- Requests accept JSON files as attachments.

## Changed

- The Improvements & suggestions button turns orange while its card is open.
- Print-setting tiles show the icon next to the label, leaving the full width to the value.
- New icon for Downloads in the sidebar; the coffee buttons wear the Ko-fi mark.
- Your profile banner shows your TigerSquad level; the Share button moved to the end of the social links.
- Clearer colours and hover effects on the request topics.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where rows of the inventory table had different heights depending on their picture.
- Fixed an issue where the Buy Me a Coffee cup was hard to see on dark backgrounds.

## Removed

- The random welcome line above your name in the sidebar.
