A small update with a fix for Creality printers and a few quality-of-life changes.

## Improvements

- Erasing a TigerTag or recycling it to a plain NFC tag now opens the guided chip window: you can see whether the right chips are on the readers, the Erase button stays disabled until they are, and a progress bar follows the write.
- The chip window no longer changes size while you use it; messages now replace the instruction line.
- Red buttons that must be held (Delete, stop a print, delete a printer or rack, …) now show how long to hold them when you hover.
- "Remove from inventory" in a spool's toolbox is now "Delete", matching the Delete button of the selection bar.
- Choosing how many spools to duplicate is now a dropdown (1–9, or 10+ to type a number, up to 50).

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the nozzle temperatures set on a Creality CFS filament slot were not saved by the printer.
- Fixed an issue where a Creality filament slot lost its material profile after being edited from the app.
