The official Cloud + LAN firmware for the FlashForge Creator 5, and spool photos framed in their own colour.

## Added

- **FlashForge Creator 5 / 5 Pro firmware** — the official "Cloud + LAN" firmware FlashForge built with TigerTag now shows up in the printer's info window: whether your printer is up to date, a download for your exact model, and the USB install guide. You get a notification when a newer version is out.
- **Long spool names scroll** — hover a card in the grid and a name too long for it scrolls into view.

## Changed

- Spool photos are now framed in the spool's colour everywhere: grid, lists, wishlist, catalogue, spool and product details. The separate colour dots are gone.
- Two-colour spools are now split on the diagonal instead of down the middle.
- On a TigerData spool, the colour is changed from a "Change colour" button next to the aspect.
- Photos wait while you scroll or move the colour filter, then appear already loaded instead of flickering in.
- The product card groups brand, material, name, aspects and weight in one block.
- In the spool details, the master spool section now comes before the weight.
- The master spool edit buttons are always visible.
- FlashForge: the connection field is now called "Printer ID", as on the printer's screen, with hints matching the setup guide.
- The network scan's add button simply reads "Add".

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where an empty grey square showed on grid cards before the photo had loaded.
- Fixed an issue where the image link bar in the spool details stayed open after confirming.
- Fixed an issue where editing a spool scrolled its details back to the top.
