## 🌙 Dark mode is here

The one you kept asking for. Tiger Studio now ships **dark by default** — every screen, every panel, every modal, redrawn for it. Prefer the old look? **Light is one click away**, in *Edit profile → Theme*, and your choice follows your account onto every machine you sign in from.

This was not a filter thrown over the app. Every colour in the interface was rebuilt on a single palette, which is why the two themes each look deliberate rather than one being a washed-out copy of the other.

## ✨ "My profile" rebuilt

Editing your profile now slides in from the left, right where your account lives, instead of a box landing in the middle of the screen.

- Everything is one clean line per setting — name, links, theme, language, country, prices.
- Your avatar colours sit with your avatar, on a single row.
- Your social links are **listed**, each showing the platform it points to, instead of hiding behind an "add" button. A note now tells you plainly that they are visible to your friends and on your public profile.
- Pressing **Enter** after pasting a link saves it and moves on.

## 🔌 Windows: run it from a USB key

A **portable** build now ships beside the installer — no installation, for machines where you are not allowed to install anything. It keeps your accounts, cache and logs **on the key** rather than on the host computer, so you leave nothing behind on a shared PC and find your inventory again on the next machine.
