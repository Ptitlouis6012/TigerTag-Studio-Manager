The camera view stops being a grid. Place your cameras where you want them, at the size you want them, and send one to a genuine fullscreen.

## Changed

- The **camera view** now works like the storage and printer boards: drag each camera where you want it on a surface you can pan and zoom, and **resize it freely** by its corner. The old ½× / 1× / 2× sizes are gone — three answers to a question you can now answer exactly.
- Added an **Arrange** mode to the camera view, with the same grid, magnetism and alignment guides as the other two boards: cameras snap flush to their neighbours, thin lines show why, and you can sweep a selection to move several at once.
- A camera **keeps its place and its size when its printer goes quiet**. It stays where you put it, dimmed and empty, and can still be moved and resized while the machine is off.
- Moving a camera no longer opens its printer's page, so laying the view out no longer interrupts itself.
- A camera sent **fullscreen now fills the whole screen**, not just the app window.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the app closed with an error dialog on an Intel Mac as soon as a camera started. The Mac download contained a video component built for Apple Silicon machines only.
- Fixed an issue where the fullscreen button could not bring a camera back out of fullscreen.
