Tell us everything from inside the app: report a problem, suggest an idea or fix a product sheet, with annotated screenshots — and follow each request until it is done.

## Added

- Improvements & suggestions: a new card, opened from the sidebar, to send a request without leaving the app and without a GitHub account — report a problem, suggest an idea or an improvement, ask a question, fix a product sheet in the catalogue, or suggest a missing brand.
- Attach up to five files: capture the app window in one click, drop or paste images, or add a PDF. The card stays open in the background, so you can close it, go to another screen and capture that one too.
- A screenshot editor opens on every capture: arrows, lines, boxes, circles, pen, highlighter, text, numbered steps, and a blur tool to hide anything private. Every mark can be moved, resized, recoloured or deleted until you are done.
- Catalogue corrections name the exact material, found with the catalogue search; brand suggestions ask for the brand's website so it can be checked.
- My requests: every request you sent, with its status (sent, in progress, done, not planned), a link to it, and a button to close your own. A notification tells you when one of them changes.
- Downloads in the sidebar, opening the download page in your language.
- Creality printers: an optional camera address in the printer settings, for custom setups.

## Changed

- The bottom of the sidebar is redesigned: Resources and Support the project sections, coloured icons, smoother hover effects, and Settings next to the version number.
- The spool card, the catalogue card and the product card now share the same icons for print settings and details.
- The product card shows the colour's hex code under Colour, in the product details.
- The view switcher now reads Inventory, Favorites, Lists, Catalogue, Printers, and its labels appear immediately on hover.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the camera of a Creality K1, K1C or K1 Max stayed black.
- Fixed an issue where the camera of an Anycubic printer connected through the cloud could not be started from the printers board, and where the camera wall kept showing "no camera" after the printer came online.

## Removed

- The mobile apps QR code in the sidebar — the new Downloads entry leads to the apps.
