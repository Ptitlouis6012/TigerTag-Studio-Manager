## Highlights

### A new kind of spool: TigerData+

Until now a spool was one of two things. Either it carried a **TigerTag** chip, and the app knew what it was. Or it was a **TigerData** — fully digital, no chip, no hardware — and it only knew what you had typed into it.

**TigerData+** closes that gap. It is a fully digital spool, with nothing to buy and nothing to stick on, that is nonetheless tied to a **real product in the official catalogue** — the exact brand, colour, material, temperatures, diameter, SKU and EAN, straight from the source rather than from your memory. It is not a TigerTag+ and never pretends to be one: no chip, no UID, and its own badge so the two can't be confused. It is the digital tier finally knowing what it is holding.

This is what the rest of the release is built around.

### Search the official catalogue, without leaving Studio

A new **Search** view browses the whole official TigerTag+ product catalogue — grid or list, with filters for type, brand, series and material, exactly like the public catalogue page. Selecting a product opens its product card with the real specs: colours, nozzle / bed / dryer temperatures, diameter, SKU, EAN and a link to its catalogue page.

From there, a single **+** turns it into a TigerData+ in your inventory, already filled in — nothing to type. Adding one never happens by accident: clicking a product only opens its card.

The catalogue is downloaded once and kept on your machine, so searching and filtering are instant and work offline. It refreshes itself daily, and **Settings → Tools** has a *Re-sync the catalogue* button, along with a line telling you how many products you hold and how old your copy is.

Every product type is browsable. For now only **filament** can be added — the field layout for resin and the other types isn't finalised yet, and the app tells you so rather than creating something half-filled.

## Added

- **A new app icon** across macOS, Windows and Linux.
- The **+** button that adds another spool of a filament is now on the spool detail card too, next to the same shopping button — the three cards that describe one product finally offer the same actions.
- A **shopping button** on the product and spool cards: green when that filament has a buy link, and clicking it opens the shop. No link yet? Clicking takes you straight to the field where you'd add one.
- When several identical spools are stacked together, each one now shows **when it was added** — "just now", "3 days ago", "2 months ago" — with the exact date on hover. Handy when the only thing telling two identical spools apart is which one you bought last.
- Two more spool containers to weigh against: **Inslogic Masterspool Black** (232 g) and **Jayo Plastic Black** (127 g).
- Importing a `.ttag` file now **refuses records that couldn't produce a working chip** instead of importing them half-filled. Refused spools are counted and shown to you, never silently dropped — and a twin pair is all-or-nothing, so you never end up with half of one.

## Changed

- Long lists now show they can scroll. Dropdown lists get a slim scrollbar back, and every list view fades softly at the top and bottom edge when there is more above or below.
- The container picker lists brands **alphabetically**, with the customizable containers kept at the top.
- The "grid view" button is a proper icon everywhere, instead of a text symbol.
- Links to the material catalogue and the shop now point at the new **tigersystem.io** addresses.

## Fixed

- Fixed the shop button not appearing on a friend's spool when that filament has a buy link.
- Fixed an issue where adding a spool from a product card left you with no sign of it — the stack of spools for that filament now opens on your new one.
- Fixed a product card showing different information depending on whether you opened it from Search or from your Favorites. It's the same card, so it now shows the same thing.
- Fixed the link to a product's catalogue page being hidden on spools without an NFC chip.
- Fixed the Favorites and Search lists losing their column header, and squaring off their rounded corners, while scrolling.
- Fixed the Search views not responding to the mouse wheel.
- Fixed printer-list checkboxes sliding over the column header while scrolling.
- Fixed the Search view being offered while browsing a friend's inventory, where adding a spool isn't possible.
- Fixed catalogue thumbnails staying blank while loading, or when a product photo is unavailable — the filament colour now shows instead.
