## Highlights

### Scan a barcode, land on the spool

Point a barcode scanner at a filament box and the catalogue opens that product — one result, not a list of look-alikes whose codes happen to share a few digits. Typing works the same way: a SKU, an EAN, a series name, a colour name, a finish like "silk" or "translucent", or simply "1 kg" now all find what you meant.

Searching by weight used to be quietly wrong: "1000" returned a few hundred products, matched through codes that happen to contain the digits rather than through their actual weight. It now returns the spools that really weigh a kilo, and "0.5 kg" and "500 g" find the same ones.

### The same filament is no longer managed twice

A TigerData is now filed together with its TigerTag, and a TigerData+ with its TigerTag+, and their product details are merged into one. The buy link, the price, the minimum stock, the favourite mark and the note are set once and apply to every version of that filament, and "in stock" counts them together — previously the same filament could show a different stock count and a different favourite depending on which one you opened.

They remain distinct where it matters: in the grid and the list each one is shown on its own, with a single clear badge, because a spool with a chip and a spool without are not interchangeable — only one of them can be written, weighed on a TigerScale or proven.

### A much bigger catalogue

The catalogue bundled with the app went from about 3 200 to nearly 5 000 products across 32 brands.

## Changed

- **Scan or type a barcode** to open a product directly; SKU, series, colour name, finish and product type are all searchable.
- **Weight searches are accurate** — "1 kg", "1000" and "500 g" now match the actual weight instead of codes that contain those digits.
- **A TigerData is filed with its TigerTag** (and a TigerData+ with its TigerTag+), their product details merged, so nothing has to be kept up to date twice — while each is still shown on its own in the grid and the list.
- **Price, buy link, minimum stock, favourites and notes follow the filament**, so they are shared by every version of it.
- **Series and colour names come straight from the catalogue** — a colour containing a dash, like "CMYK - Magenta", is no longer cut in the wrong place.
- The bundled catalogue grew to nearly **5 000 products**.

## Fixed

- Fixed a spool added from an existing one briefly showing a second, wrong badge on its card.
- Fixed products without a photo being drawn as a generic spool sketch instead of their own colour, in the grid, the list, the details panel and the product card.
