## Added

- The product card now shows the material's **colours** (one hex per line, so a dual or tri-colour lists all of them) and its **quantity** the way the product states it — `2 kg` rather than `2000 g`.
- The bundled catalogue grew to **7 552 products**, so Search finds far more on a fresh install, before any sync.
- **Refill**, **Recycled** and **Filled** now appear as badges on both the spool card and the product card.

## Fixed

- Fixed the product card always showing a **1 kg** spool: a 500 g, 750 g or 2 kg product now shows its real size. This affected every product card in the app.
- Fixed a long **SKU** or barcode running off the side of a card — and a shorter one wrapping onto two lines while half the row sat empty.
