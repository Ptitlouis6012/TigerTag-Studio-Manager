The TigerSquad gets its rankings: see where your country and you stand among the makers, every way to earn XP, and the history of your points. Joining and levelling up now come with sound and a proper celebration.

## Added

- TigerSquad rankings with four tabs: the Countries League (members' XP added up per country), the Makers (every member by XP, with level and flag), Achievements (what you have earned, what is left, and exactly what it takes to reach the next level) and History (every gain and loss with its date).
- Your world rank ("#3 worldwide of 9") in My profile, the Support menu and the Improvements & suggestions card, and a rank chip next to your XP in the sidebar.
- "TigerSquad Ranking" (members) or "Join the TigerSquad" (everyone else) in the account menu.
- New ways to earn XP: friends, social links, a profile photo, a public profile, connected printers, a TigerScale, a TigerSpool, the phone app, a TigerPOD, an RFID reader, a TD1S sensor, your first rack and your first list. Opening each community link in the sidebar (wiki, Discord, GitHub, MakerWorld, shop, support) is worth 5 XP.
- Notifications when you gain XP (grouped per day) and when you reach a new level.
- A level-up window with a celebration when you climb from Bronze to Silver, Gold or Platinum — shown right away, or the next time you open the app.
- Sounds and a celebration when the TigerSquad invitation appears and when you join; a light runs round the Join button when you hover it.
- Choose the country you represent in the Countries League from My profile (your detected country by default).

## Changed

- TigerSquad members wear their level badge on their avatar instead of the heart, and their own avatar in the sidebar shows its level ring. Platinum now uses the blue of its crystals.
- More space between friends in the sidebar, so level rings and badges no longer overlap.
- The TigerSquad invitation shows after three days of use, about ten seconds after you open the app or switch accounts, and comes back the next day if you close it.
- The empty Printers screen shows a line-up of printers from six brands, and lists all seven supported brands.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where your avatar, the buttons and your friends shifted for a moment while the sidebar folded or unfolded.
