## ➕ Adding a material now asks where it comes from

The **+ Material** button opens a card with the two ways in, instead of dropping you straight into a blank form:

- **From Catalogue** — find the real product and everything is filled in for you. Opens the catalogue as a grid, so you recognise the spool by its photo and its colour.
- **Manually** — not in the catalogue? Fill it in yourself.

Each choice shows what it will produce, now and once you burn it onto a chip: **TigerData+ / TigerTag+** from the catalogue, **TigerData / TigerTag** by hand. Hover the ⓘ on a line for the details.

The card leaves the rest of the app visible and clickable behind it, and if you open a spool or a printer while it is up, the new card pushes it aside instead of hiding it. The separate "From Catalogue" button in the toolbar is gone — it lives in this card now.

## ⚖️ The TigerScale card is now the V3

New photo, new icon, and the description finally matches the machine you can build today: two NFC readers, real remaining weight, the large touchscreen with its calibration wizard, sync to your account, brand and material recognised without an internet connection, and a scale that runs on its own — no computer to switch on, no app to open.

A **Get it on MakerWorld** button sits beside "View on GitHub" for the printable body, and the GitHub button no longer sends you to the discontinued V2 repository.

## Fixed

- Fixed printer photos appearing as black silhouettes in the printers list in dark mode — permanently on Windows, and when hovering a row on macOS.
- Fixed the "Windows Hello for Business" smart-card reader, which Windows creates on its own on managed work PCs, being detected as a third NFC reader. It added an empty slot to the burn window that could never turn green, which left the Burn button disabled.
