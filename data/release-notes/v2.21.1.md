# v2.21.1

Emergency fix for anyone using the new **rack depth**. Please update.

## Fixed

- Fixed spools being thrown out of their racks on launch. If a rack held spools two or three rows deep, everything behind the front row came back as "not stored" a couple of seconds after the app opened — and because that happened behind the *What's New* window, it looked as though closing that window had caused it. Your arrangement was being erased for real, not just on screen.
- Fixed one half of a twin-tagged spool (one spool, two chips) being thrown out of its slot at random on launch. This one predates rack depth; depth simply made it show.
- Added a safety net behind both: the checks that tidy up spools sharing a slot, or pointing at a deleted rack, now refuse to act when they would empty a large part of your storage, and say so instead. A tidy-up is a handful of spools — never half your collection.
