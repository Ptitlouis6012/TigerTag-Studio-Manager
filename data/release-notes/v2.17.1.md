## Fixed

- Fixed **Sign in with Google**, which had been failing since v1.10.0. The app now completes the sign-in in your own browser as intended, instead of falling back to an embedded window that Google refuses ("browser not compatible with JavaScript"). If you were stuck signing in with Google, this release unblocks you.

## Changed

- Signing in with Google is much quicker when you are already signed in to Google in your browser: no more account chooser and full identity check on every single sign-in. If you use several Google accounts, the chooser still appears.
