Two new ways to connect printers — Klipper and OctoPrint — a search that finds your printer by name, automatic Cloud ⇄ LAN switching for Bambu Lab, and a USB scale that wakes up on its own.

## Added

- Klipper printers (beta): any printer running Klipper with Moonraker — Voron, RatRig, Vzbot, Sovol SV07 / SV08 / Zero, Qidi, Elegoo Neptune 4, Creality K1 / K2, FLSun, Artillery X4 and more. Live print progress with thumbnail and layers, pause / resume / stop, temperatures, camera, movement and homing controls, part fan, speed and lights, and the filament slots of Happy Hare and AFC changers. 161 models with pictures.
- Marlin printers through OctoPrint (beta): any Marlin printer driven by OctoPrint (OctoPi). No key to copy: Tiger Studio asks OctoPrint for access and you click Allow. Live print progress with thumbnail, pause / resume / stop, temperatures, camera, movement, homing, fan and speed. 187 models with pictures.
- Find your printer: the Add a printer card now has a search across every brand and connection at once. Pick your machine; if it can be reached more than one way (for example a Creality K1, through Creality or Klipper), you choose which one. The model and its name are filled in for you.
- Bambu Lab: a Cloud / LAN switch in the settings of printers added through your Bambu account. Tiger Studio also switches on its own when the current connection stops answering and the other one works, keeps the printer's name and access code in step with your Bambu account, and tells you when the printer is no longer linked to your account.
- USB scale (DYMO): a "Weigh again" button to take a fresh reading without lifting the spool.
- New achievement: connecting a USB scale earns 100 XP.
- New printers: Elegoo Centauri 2, Centauri 2 Combo and ELEGOO × emoji® Centauri Carbon 2 Combo. Prusa printers and the Elegoo Centauri Carbon now show their picture.

## Changed

- The model list in a printer's settings has a search field.
- Beta printers now show a small Beta tag with an explanation on hover, instead of a banner.
- Messages stay on screen long enough to be read.
- Updated product catalogue.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where a DYMO USB scale was no longer detected after going to sleep until it was unplugged and plugged back in.
- Fixed an issue where the Elegoo Centauri Carbon camera did not show.
- Fixed an issue where the Elegoo camera showed a message about FlashForge's single-viewer limit.
- Fixed an issue where a Bambu Lab camera could keep reconnecting and never show.
- Fixed an issue where error messages from printers were never displayed.
- Fixed an issue where the Add a printer card stayed on top of another card you had just opened.
- Fixed an issue where scrolling a printer's card could cut its header in half.
