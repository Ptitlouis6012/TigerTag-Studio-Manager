Bambu Lab and Creality chips linked from every way of adding a spool, your TigerCoins always in the top bar, a colour filter in the catalogue picker, and a clearer TigerCoins history.

## Added

- **Link your Bambu Lab / Creality chips when you add a spool.** With such chips on the TigerPOD, "Add from the catalogue" and "Add manually" offer "Link the proprietary third-party Tags" instead of a burn: the spool is created and the chips are linked to it. These chips are never written.
- **The buttons follow what is on the TigerPOD.** Swap Bambu Lab chips for a blank tag (or the other way round) while the "+" window is open: "Link" becomes "Burn" and back, without leaving the page you are on.
- **Search by chip UID.** The inventory search finds a spool by the UID of a Bambu Lab / Creality chip linked to it, whole or partial.
- **TigerCoins in the top bar.** Your balance, the envelopes waiting and the gift-code button are always visible for TigerSquad members; each opens the Deals page with the right window.
- **Colour filter in the catalogue picker.** "Add from the catalogue" has its own small colour bars, separate from the inventory's.
- "Works with" caption over the brand logos of the "+" window.

## Changed

- **TigerCoins history:** an envelope received shows in green with "+1", one given in red with "−1", with your friend's avatar; coins earned in gold.
- Every envelope can be given to a friend, including the ones from TigerTag+ chips. The "Give" window always opens, with today's count.
- Opening a deal closes the other side cards.
- While the "+" window is open, a spool's card no longer offers the upgrade to TigerData+.
- The "Link to my inventory" list of the "+" window also searches your tags.
- With the NFC tag tester open, placing a chip no longer opens or adds a spool.
- With a single RFID reader, its plate shows its number like with two.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where deleting a spool left its Bambu Lab / Creality chips marked as linked.
- Fixed an issue where a Bambu Lab / Creality chip placed while the "+" window was open was ignored, or led to a burn.
- Fixed an issue where a TigerData spool with linked Bambu Lab / Creality chips offered to burn a TigerTag+.
- Fixed an issue where "Upgrade to TigerData+" did nothing after closing the "+" window.
- Fixed an issue where the TigerCoins history opened small and resized itself.
