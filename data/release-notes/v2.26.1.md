A quick follow-up to v2.26.0: Bambu Lab printers added with your account now show what they are printing, wherever you are.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where a Bambu Lab printer added through your account never showed the print's preview picture unless you were on the same network as the machine — which rather defeated the point of adding it through the cloud.
- Fixed an issue where the preview and the print's name disappeared the moment a job finished, leaving a stock photo of the printer in their place. A cloud printer now keeps showing what it last made, and what it was called.
- Fixed an issue where the preview picture blinked out and back every few seconds on cloud printers.
