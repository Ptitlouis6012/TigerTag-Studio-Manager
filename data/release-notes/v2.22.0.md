Your printers now live on a board you arrange yourself — with their filament units, their temperatures, and everything staying where you put it.

## Added

- The Printers view is now a board you lay out by hand, like Storage. Place each machine where it stands in your workshop, zoom in and out, and pan around. Your arrangement is saved to your account.
- A connected machine's filament storage now appears as separate units — Ext., AMS 1, AMS 2 — instead of one flat strip of slots. They can be kept together beside the machine or split apart and placed individually, so an X1C with two AMS and a spool arm looks like three boxes standing in three places.
- Filament slots on the board show what each bay holds: its colour and its material, at the same size as a slot in your Storage racks. An empty bay stays visible as an outline, and a bay assigned a colour but not yet loaded shows as a ring so it can't be mistaken for a full one.
- A temperature widget can be shown beside each machine. It stays on the board when the printer is off, showing a dash for each reading, so your layout never rearranges itself behind your back.
- Cards can be bound together for good. Select a few, right-click, group — from then on, moving one moves them all, at the spacing you gave them. Right-click again to release them where they stand.
- Each machine's ⋮ menu lets you show or hide its widgets. A widget you switch on appears right under its own machine, and switching it off then on again brings it back beside the machine rather than wherever it last was.
- Sweep an empty area of either board to select several machines or racks at once, hold Shift to add more, then drag any one of them to move the whole set.
- Every card has a move handle next to its ⋮. Outside Arrange mode, that handle is the only thing that moves it — so clicking a machine opens it, and a rack no longer slides because you reached past its title.

## Changed

- Printers are no longer split into "Connected" and "Offline" sections. A machine losing its connection stays exactly where you put it — its status is already shown by the coloured dot on its card.
- Arrange mode and the zoom controls now float over the top-right corner of the board instead of taking up a row above it.
- Clicking a machine whose panel is already open now closes it.
- Middle-click and drag to pan a board; the left button now draws a selection instead.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where updating a spool's chip from the app erased that spool's own information the next time it was read: the weight went back to the full spool weight, and the masterspool, the rack and the TD value were lost. The chip's creation date can no longer be overwritten by an update — it is also what pairs a twin-tagged spool's two chips together.
- Fixed an issue where a spool's TD value disappeared from the app when its entry was rebuilt from the chip, even though the chip still carried the measurement. It is now read back from the chip.
- Fixed an issue where switching to the Printers view did nothing at all, and could stay stuck that way.
- Fixed an issue where moving a group of cards made them flash back to their previous position before landing, and where a card being dragged could jump somewhere else entirely while a machine reconnected.
- Fixed an issue where turning a widget on or off closed the machine's menu, so showing two widgets meant opening the menu twice.
- Fixed an issue where a group's outline stayed behind other machines' cards when the group was moved over them.
- Fixed an issue where the board jumped back to its top-left corner every time it redrew, losing wherever you had panned to.
- Fixed an issue where grouping cards nudged them out of place.
- Fixed an issue where the temperature widget was left behind when its group moved.
- Fixed an issue where Arrange mode, a right-click, or the ⋮ button opened a machine's panel when it shouldn't.
- Fixed an issue where a selection sweep leaving the board left its box frozen on screen, and where a card dragged past the edge kept following the cursor instead of being dropped.
- Fixed an issue where a card being dragged gave no visible sign it had been picked up, and where the closed-hand cursor only appeared after the card had already moved.
- Fixed an issue where the overlap warning on the Storage board hid the drag shadow, and did not show at all outside Arrange mode.
- Fixed an issue where an Anycubic printer showed a dash instead of zero for a temperature target it had not reported.
