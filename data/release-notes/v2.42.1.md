Your public profile is now saved and checked by the server, the Elegoo Centauri Carbon shows its Canvas slots, and Tiger Touch gets a new icon.

## Changed

- Your pseudo, avatar colour, photo, links, profile visibility and friend code are now saved and checked by the TigerTag server. When a change is refused, Tiger Studio tells you why instead of silently dropping it.
- Pseudo rules: 2 to 40 characters, no link or e-mail address, up to three changes a day.
- An account with no pseudo, no valid language or no avatar is asked to finish its setup when it signs in.
- A new Tiger Touch icon in the top bar.
- Anycubic printers connected through the Anycubic cloud can now be controlled from a Tiger Touch (pause, resume, stop, light).
- Updated product catalogue.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where changes to your public profile (pseudo, colour, photo, links, visibility) were not saved.
- Fixed an issue where a friend's avatar could lose its colour.
- Fixed an issue where the Canvas of an Elegoo Centauri Carbon showed a single unknown slot instead of its four spools. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012.
