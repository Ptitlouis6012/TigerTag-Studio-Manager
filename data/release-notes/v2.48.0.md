Tiger Studio Manager 2: a new welcome, a guided first start, one window for the whole TigerSystem, and your account emails now come from TigerSystem.

## Added

- **"Where do we start?"** With no spool yet, three cards take you to a first win: add a spool, plug in your TigerPOD, connect a printer. The TigerPOD card turns green as soon as a reader is plugged in. The Printers view shows the same screen while you have no printer.
- **"Going further"** After your first spool, a small optional strip offers the TigerPOD and a real printer, each with "I don't have one", and goes away once both are done or skipped.
- **One window for the whole ecosystem.** TigerPOD, TigerScale, TigerSpool, Tiger Touch, TigerNFC and TigerTag each get a sheet: what it does, what it is for, what you need, and how to get one, with the guide and the download links. The sheets play in turn and pause while your mouse is on the window.
- **"Check your inbox"** After creating an account with an email address, a clear step shows where the confirmation was sent, lets you send it again or use another address.

## Changed

- The app is now **Tiger Studio Manager 2**, with a new welcome page: an animated view of the devices, the printer brands it works with, and clear "Create my account" / "I already have an account" buttons.
- On first launch, the app opens in your computer's language when it is one of the 11 supported.
- Account emails (address confirmation, password reset) now come from TigerSystem, in your language.
- The TigerPOD presented is the TigerPOD Mini: one printed part and two ACR122U readers.
- "Get the app" for Tiger NFC Connect opens the TigerSystem download page.
- New 3D printer icon and the official Klipper logo.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where adding a spool by hand with an RFID reader plugged in only offered to burn a TigerTag, with no way to just create the spool (#161). With a reader and no chip, both choices are now shown.
- Fixed an issue where unplugging the RFID reader left the "Burn the TigerTag" buttons on screen.
- Fixed an issue where the "From the catalogue" list flickered on every letter typed.
- Fixed an issue where a TigerData spool with linked Bambu Lab / Creality chips did not offer to burn a TigerTag.
- Fixed an issue where the eye button of the password field did not show the password.
