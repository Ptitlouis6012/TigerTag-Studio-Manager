Link the RFID chips of your Bambu Lab and Creality spools, add any IP camera to your camera wall, and a smoother way to add or burn a material.

## Added

- **Link the chips already on your spools.** Bambu Lab and Creality spools carry their maker's RFID chips; other MIFARE Classic chips (QIDI Box, Snapmaker U1…) should work too. Place one on the TigerPOD and link its chips to a material of your inventory, or add it from the catalogue or by hand. From then on, placing that spool opens it, like a TigerTag. The link is made by the chip's ID only: nothing is written into the maker's chips. A held "Unlink" removes it.
- **One window to add a material.** Placing a blank tag or an unknown chip, or pressing "+" in the search bar, opens the same window: Add from the catalogue, Add manually, or Link to my inventory, side by side.
  - The catalogue opens straight away; picking a product shows its card with "+ Inventory".
  - "Link to my inventory" shows your materials as in the inventory grid, with a brand filter. With a blank tag, it lists the materials not yet on a chip, ready to be burned.
- **Burn in one gesture.** Hold "Burn the TigerTag" (or TigerTag+) on a material's card: when the chips are in place, the burn starts by itself.
- **External IP cameras.** Add any IP camera (RTSP, MJPEG or WebRTC) from "Add a 3D printer" → External cameras, with a Test button. It sits on the camera wall and on the printers board like a printer's camera: a workshop camera, a second view of a printer, or the one watching your front door.
- **TigerCoins history.** Click your balance on the Deals banner to see every coin won, spent and given.
