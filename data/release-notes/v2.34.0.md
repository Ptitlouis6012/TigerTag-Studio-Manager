Meet TigerSpool, the RFID reader for every 3D printer. Also new: printers that follow a new IP on their own, multi-select in the catalogue, and AI assistants that work from the web.

## Added

- TigerSpool support: a new icon in the header shows each of your TigerSpool readers (active, screen off or offline, with battery level). Click it to see each reader's status, Wi-Fi, firmware and the printers it serves, and whether it can reach each one.
- Without a TigerSpool yet, the same icon presents the product, with the printable files on MakerWorld (spool on the left or on the right), the wiki and the source code.
- Printers now reconnect by themselves after their IP address changes: when a printer on your network can't be reached, the app looks for it on the network, saves its new address and reconnects. Works with Bambu Lab, Creality, Elegoo, FlashForge, Snapmaker and Anycubic printers on the local network.
- Catalogue: select several products (tick boxes in the table, or Select in the grid, with Shift-click for a range) and add them to favorites, to your order list or to a list in one go.
- "Add to a list" now works on a whole selection of materials, favorites or catalogue products.
- My profile → AI assistants: connect Claude.ai, ChatGPT and other assistants from the web or your phone, with nothing to install and without the app running. The profile shows the address to use, a link to the guide, and the online assistants connected to your account, each with a button to cut its access.

## Changed

- Print progress bars now show how a print ended: blue while printing, green when finished, red when it failed and grey when cancelled. The bar keeps the last progress instead of emptying.
- YouTube and Google Drive videos on a product now play inside the app instead of opening the browser.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where adding a material from a product didn't offer to write the chip when an RFID reader was plugged in.
