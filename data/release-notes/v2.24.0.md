Your printer board gains three things it was missing: the print in progress, the machine's camera, and the pause and stop buttons — right on the card, without opening anything.

## Added

- Added a **Print** widget to the printer board: the preview of what is on the bed, the file name, the percentage and its bar, the state, the time remaining, the finish time and the layer count. Place it wherever you like, like any other widget.
- Added a **Camera** widget, and a camera on the machine's own card. Hover a printer's photo and press play — the photo gives way to the live feed. It is off until you ask for it, so a board full of machines does not open a stream for each of them.
- Added **pause and stop** on a machine's card while it is printing, next to its photo. Stop asks you to hold it for a moment: it ends a print that may be hours old, and a board shows many machines at once.

## Changed

- The widget switches in a machine's ⋮ menu are now ordered Print, Temperature, Filament storage, Camera.
- A group's outline now appears only while you are moving something. At rest the board shows your machines rather than the rings around them; during a move every ring appears, so you can see what is coming with you and what you are about to land among.
- The filament widget now appears even for a printer that has never connected, so you can place it before the machine has said anything.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where a camera showed in one view and stayed black in another, or went black seconds after opening correctly. A feed is now shared properly between the board, the camera wall and the printer's own page — one connection to the machine, whichever views are open.
- Fixed an issue where a camera went black for several seconds every time you changed view, even though the app was showing it a moment earlier.
- Fixed an issue where a camera that failed to start was never tried again, leaving that view black until the app was restarted.
- Fixed an issue where a FlashForge printer's camera could stay reserved by a view you had already left, so no other view could show it.
- Fixed an issue where a widget changed width depending on where it stood on the board, folding its contents onto two rows near the right edge.
- Fixed an issue where a widget switched on for a printer that had never connected would not follow its machine when moved, and could not be selected with a sweep.
- Fixed an issue where the colour filter was drawn over the camera wall, offering to pick a spool colour on a screen that holds no spools.
