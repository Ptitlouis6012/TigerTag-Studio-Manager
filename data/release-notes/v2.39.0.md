A lighter, smoother Tiger Studio: much less CPU when the app sits idle, photos that fade instead of blinking while you scroll, and a bigger look at any thumbnail by just hovering it.

## Added

- Hover a thumbnail in the inventory, catalogue or list tables to see a larger preview of the spool or product, framed in its colour.
- Requests sent from the app now include your TigerSquad level and XP, so the team sees who is reporting.

## Changed

- While you scroll the catalogue or the inventory, photos fade out and back in smoothly instead of disappearing and popping back; the TigerTag logo crossfades with them.
- TigerSquad rankings show ranks as #1, #2…
- "TigerTag+ Certified" is now used everywhere in the TigerSquad texts.
- New brand: IEMAI.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where Tiger Studio used a lot of CPU while sitting idle (around half a core on the inventory), making fans spin up — especially on Linux.
- Fixed an issue where a profile photo that failed to load left an empty circle instead of showing the initials.

## Removed

- The FlashForge "Adventurer A5" model, which does not exist in FlashForge's range.
