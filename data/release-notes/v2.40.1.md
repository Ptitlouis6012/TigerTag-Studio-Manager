A cleaner XP history, Tiger Studio in the browser that feels at home on an iPad, and a steadier printers board.

## Changed

- Tiger Studio in the browser now lives at studio.tigersystem.io, with its own sign-in that it remembers. The Google sign-in window now shows tigersystem.io.
- The browser version adapts to iPad and touch screens: the sidebar slims down in portrait and opens up in landscape, and actions that used to need a mouse hover are always visible.
- Updated brands and product catalogue (new brand: Filament 3D).

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where some XP gains appeared twice in the XP history (for example "+20" twice for one printer). Your XP total was always correct; the duplicate entries have been removed.
- Fixed an issue where the browser version was marked "Not secure" and asked for access to devices on your local network.
- Fixed an issue where printer cards could pile up in the top-left corner of the printers board after a long drag, with the new position not saved.
- Fixed an issue where a printer card could be dragged far off the printers board and seem to disappear.
- Fixed an issue where the "Hold to confirm" hint stayed on screen after deleting a printer.
