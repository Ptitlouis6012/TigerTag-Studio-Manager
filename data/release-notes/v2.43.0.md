Tiger Quests arrive in Tiger Studio: daily quests, tutorial missions and the TigerSquad rankings, plus a full XP history, photos and videos from your printer cameras, and cameras that can start on their own.

## Added

- **Tiger Quests.** A new Tiger Quests section in the sidebar and the view bar.
  - **Daily quests:** open the app and complete four small missions drawn each day for XP. Completing all of them opens a bonus chest, and every five full days in a row adds a streak bonus.
  - **Tutorial missions:** levels of five missions each (Discovery and Explorer) that walk you through the app, with an illustrated card and a bonus per level.
- **TigerSquad rankings.** A new TigerSquad view with three leagues side by side: countries, your own country and makers worldwide. Countries are shown with their flag, and the top three get a gold, silver or bronze ring. Arrows show how many places each member moved since the start of the day. The Community pulse shows the total XP, spools and printers of the community, updated live.
- **TigerSquad items.** Every XP item is listed on a rarity ladder, with the ones you own lit up.
- **XP history page.** Your total XP, the XP gained and lost and your activities over the last 30 days. It includes your XP balance over 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or all time, the XP breakdown by category, and the full activity log with filters and search.
- **Camera photos and videos.** Hover any printer camera (side panel or camera wall) to take a photo, record a video (up to 10 minutes) or open the printer's capture folder. Photos keep the camera's full resolution. Captures are filed per printer and stay together even after the printer changes IP address or name.
- **Start the camera automatically.** A new option in each printer's settings: once the printer is online, its camera starts on the printers board without a click.
- **Klipper and OctoPrint add flow** in clear steps: connector, printer model, then Scan or IP address, then the settings.

## Changed

- Your XP and world rank are now shown in the profile header, next to the Share button, and open the rankings.
- Creality side panel: the print card now always sits under the camera, like on Snapmaker. The disable motors, files and light buttons are grouped in the Control block.
- Settings → Data: "Export .ttag" saves your whole inventory in the TigerTag file format.
- Demo printers no longer earn XP.
- The catalogue grid stays fast with the full catalogue of more than 18,000 products.
- A product card opens instantly from the catalogue.
- Klipper: a "Custom port" option for printers that do not use the standard Moonraker port.
- Bambu Lab H2S, H2D, H2D Pro, H2C and X2D: the light button controls both chamber lights. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012.
- Elegoo Centauri Carbon: the Canvas trays are now shown in the side panel too. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012.
- New Tiger Touch icon in the top bar. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where a Creality camera kept flashing black when it was open on the printers board and in the side panel at the same time.
- Fixed an issue where a friend could send you a new friend request that could not be accepted.
- Fixed an issue where a transparent product photo was shown on a dark square in the product and spool cards.
- Fixed an issue where the Klipper part-fan percentage did not match Mainsail. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012.
- Fixed an issue where a Klipper or OctoPrint camera stayed dimmed after a failed picture. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012.
