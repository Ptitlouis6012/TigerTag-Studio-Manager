## Important — please update

This release fixes a bug that could **erase the details of spools without a chip**. On one account, 136 spools lost their brand, material, colour, weight and price in a few seconds — they stayed in the inventory, but blank, stacked together as a single pile of empty spools.

It happened while the app was renaming older chipless spools behind the scenes, and it could affect any account still holding spools created before that rename. **Updating stops it.** If your inventory already shows a stack of blank spools, get in touch — we can tell you exactly which brands and materials were affected, from your own statistics history.

## Highlights

### The catalogue is in the box

The official product catalogue — around 3 000 filaments — now ships inside the app. Search works on **first launch**, offline, instead of waiting for the first download. It still refreshes itself in the background, so what you get is a starting point, never a frozen copy.

### Your spools keep themselves current

When a brand corrects a product — a temperature, a diameter, a colour name — the spools you own are quietly brought up to date. No button to press, no list to review.

Only the spools carrying a **TigerTag+ chip** can ever ask anything of you, and only when the change concerns something written **on the chip** itself. Everything else updates in silence. Previously the app asked for a chip re-write over things the chip doesn't even hold, like a product being renamed — it no longer does.

### One rule for every spool colour

A two-colour spool used to split down the middle in one place and the opposite way in another, so a spool could visibly flip sides between the preview and your inventory. Every surface that draws a spool now follows the same rule, at the same angle, everywhere in the app.

## Added

- The product catalogue ships with the app — **searchable on first launch, offline**.
- Spools tied to a catalogue product now **keep themselves up to date** as the catalogue changes.
- Product **series and names** come straight from the catalogue, so a colour with a dash in it is no longer split in the wrong place.

## Changed

- **"To order" now sits with your Lists**, next to the wishlists that feed it, instead of with Favorites.
- **"From the catalogue"** opens in the layout you were already using — leave a grid, arrive in a grid.
- Two-colour spools, rainbows and gradients follow **one shared rule** across every view, illustration and preview.
- The TigerTag watermark on catalogue thumbnails matches every other one instead of looking washed out.

## Fixed

- Fixed spools without a chip losing their details during the background rename of older entries.
- Fixed exporting a `.ttag` file rejecting **every** spool, so nothing could be exported at all.
- Fixed being asked to re-write a chip when nothing written on that chip had actually changed.
- Fixed a bulk delete being able to reach spools hidden by the current filter — the bar now tells you when part of your selection is out of sight.
- Fixed opening a filter list pushing the whole view sideways and clipping its edge.
- Fixed the Search list losing its column header while scrolling.
- Fixed no view being highlighted in the view selector when the app opened.
- Fixed closing a product card leaving its row still highlighted behind it.
