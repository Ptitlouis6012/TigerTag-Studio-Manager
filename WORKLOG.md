# Worklog — v2.33.1 (in progress)

## Added
- Printers follow their new IP on their own: when a LAN printer gives up reconnecting (Creality/Elegoo after their 3 attempts, FlashForge/Snapmaker from the 3rd retry, Bambu/Anycubic still down 20 s after an MQTT drop), its brand's LAN scan runs silently, the printer is matched on its stable identity (serial / `sn` / MAC from the scan or the ARP cache; Creality hostname only when unique), and on exactly one match at another address the new IP is saved, the printer reconnected and a toast tells the user. Never for cloud printers or one switched offline on purpose; one scan per brand at a time; never takes an IP another printer of the brand uses; fruitless searches back off 5 min → 1 h. Verified end to end on a Snapmaker U1 given a wrong IP — `renderer/printers/refind.js` (new), `renderer/printers/{bambulab,creality,elegoo,flashforge,snapmaker,anycubic}/index.js`, `renderer/printers/context.js`, `renderer/inventory.js` (`reconnectPrinter`, `onPrinterRefound`)
- Creality / Snapmaker WebSocket connects give up after 8 s instead of the OS's ~75 s on a dead address, so retries (and the re-find) start promptly — `renderer/printers/{creality,snapmaker}/index.js`
- Printers remember who they are, not just where they are: the first time a Creality / Snapmaker / Anycubic (LAN) printer connects in a session, its stable identity is saved on the printer doc even when it was added by typing its IP — `serialNumber` (Creality hello `deviceSn` / Snapmaker product info) or `macAddress` (Anycubic `/info` usn), copied from `discovery` when present else probed once; plus `macAddress` from the OS ARP cache (new `net:arp-mac` IPC, directly attached subnets only — null across a router); Creality falls back to its per-unit `hostName` when it has neither. Groundwork for re-finding a printer after a DHCP IP change — `renderer/printers/identity.js` (new), `renderer/printers/{creality,snapmaker,anycubic}/index.js`, `renderer/printers/context.js` (`savePrinterField`), `renderer/inventory.js`, `main.js`, `preload.js`
- Catalogue (Search views) multi-select: tick products in the table column, or via Select in the grid (shift-click for a range), then ★ Favorite / 🛒 To order / Add to a list the whole selection from the shared bulk bar (with a "N picked" count; no tags / delete there). Each item's product detail is fetched once through the product-card cache, six at a time, prefetched as you tick; one action is capped at 200 products — `renderer/inventory.js` (`state.selectedCatalog`, `_bulkCtx` catalogue context, `_catResolveRow(s)`, `_catBulkClick`), `renderer/inventory.html`, `renderer/css/70-detail-misc.css`
- Bulk "Add to a list" button in the bulk bar for materials, the Favorites table and the catalogue; the list popup now takes several products at once (ticked when the list already holds them all; one arrayUnion / arrayRemove write) — `renderer/inventory.js` (`_addManyToList`, `_openAddToListMenu`), `renderer/inventory.html`, `renderer/css/70-detail-misc.css`
- TigerSpool in the header, right of the TigerScale: one glyph per box (capped at 2), green active · blue screen off · red offline, with the battery badge when a cell is fitted; hovering lists every TigerSpool by name with its status and how many printers it serves (or how long ago it went quiet). Live from `users/{uid}/tigerspools`, same online windows as the TigerScale (90 s / 11 min screen off); always shown (grey and faded with no box). A click opens a side card with each box — status, Wi-Fi, power, firmware, address — and the printers active on it, matched to the account's own printers by document id, each with a dot saying whether the BOX can reach it (`printers_online`, published by TigerSpool firmware after 1.70.0 — hollow grey 'unknown' when the box is offline or on older firmware); a row opens that printer. One card per box holding its identity, chips and printers; the card photo shows the whole box and its whole spool (`assets/img/TigerSpool_card.png`); panel 400 px wide with margins around the cards. With no box, the click opens a modal that presents the product and how to build one (wiki guide + browser installer); each card has a hold-to-confirm remove (deletes `users/{uid}/tigerspools/{mac}`, like a TigerScale — a box still on signs back in on its next beat, said in the tooltip) — `renderer/IoT/tigerspool/` (new: `index.js`, `panel.js`, `tigerspool.css`), `assets/svg/icons/icon_tigerspool_3d.svg`, `assets/img/TigerSpool.webp` (new), `renderer/inventory.html`, `renderer/inventory.js`

## Changed
- Print progress bars are one flat colour and follow the job: blue while printing, green at 100 % when finished, red when the job fails and grey when it is cancelled — both frozen at the % the bar had while it was still blue, instead of dropping to an empty bar. Same rule in the printers table, the printer cards, the board, the dashboard widget and all six brands' side cards — `renderer/printers/job-bar.js` (new), `renderer/printers/{bambulab,anycubic,flashforge,snapmaker,elegoo}/cards.js`, `renderer/printers/creality/index.js`, `renderer/inventory.js` (`_getPrinterJob` → `job.bar`, `_jobBarPct`, `_setJobFillTone`), `renderer/css/40-printers.css`, `renderer/css/50-snapmaker.css`
- Product videos play inside the card (spool detail + product card, incl. the catalogue): YouTube (watch / youtu.be / shorts / embed / live) and Google Drive links now show a poster that turns into an in-app player on click, instead of opening the browser or only listing a link; MP4 files keep playing inline. Playable videos added as product web-link attachments get the same player (deduped against the chip's own video) instead of a plain link. Both cards draw every video through one entry point `_productVideosHTML` → `_videoSectionHTML`, and the attachment "video" icon uses the same detection (`_isPlayableVideo`) — `renderer/inventory.js`, `renderer/css/70-detail-misc.css`
